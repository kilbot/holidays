## What the extra money buys — and what it doesn't

- **Seat: a tie.** China Southern 787-9 both legs: 32" pitch, 3-3-3, seatback screen, USB, shared AC (needs a converter plug). Cathay Madrid → Hong Kong A350: 32" / 18", 3-3-3, AC + USB, paid Wi-Fi; Hong Kong → Perth is the refreshed 777: **3-4-3, 31" / 17.2"** — tighter than China Southern's 787. THAI Paris 777: 31" / 18".
- **Food and screens: Cathay wins.** Flight-Report meals 8.0 vs 7.4, screens/Wi-Fi 8.5 vs 6.7. China Southern's recurring complaints: weak meals, some dead screens, slow or paid Wi-Fi.
- **Crew: Cathay wins.** World's Best Cabin Crew 2026. Skytrax on China Southern: "some issues with communication" for non-Chinese speakers.
- **Punctuality: China Southern wins** (~90 % vs ~77 %). Its forum complaints are about missed connections and lost or damaged bags when it does go wrong.
- **Schedule: Cathay wins.** Daily from Madrid; China Southern three days a week, so a missed connection can cost days.
- **The stop: Hong Kong beats Guangzhou** as a place to spend a night (see below). Guangzhou's transit is rated "well managed" by Skytrax and signed in English.
- **Bags:** all three give 1 × 23 kg on their cheapest fares (China Southern Flex: 2 × 23 kg; THAI Flexi: 30 kg). All through-check bags to Perth on one ticket.
- **Recent reviews:** China Southern Perth–Guangzhou, Sept 2026: "the amenity kit is up there with the best you will find in economy… the meals are generous and the 32-inch seat pitch sits at the upper end"; another, Apr 2026, meals 1/10. Cathay Munich–Hong Kong, June 2026: "very comfortable", 8.5/10; London–Hong Kong, Mar 2026: "my least favourite seat in Cathay's economy", lavatories poor. THAI Perth–Bangkok: food "surprisingly delicious", crew "polite but perfunctory", Wi-Fi "non-functional".

## Jet lag (Perth = Hong Kong = Guangzhou time, 7 h ahead of Spain)

- Best shape: a **night in a bed at the stopover, then an afternoon flight landing in Perth in the evening** — China Southern 12 Dec (night in Guangzhou), Cathay Pick A and Pick B all do this. ★★★
- China Southern 15 Dec: day flight out, 7.5 h awake in Guangzhou, land 21:35 — tired but you go straight to bed. ★★
- THAI via Bangkok: 18 h awake in Bangkok, then a night flight landing 07:55 — the first Perth day is lost to sleep. ★★
- Avoid: sleeping all day at the hub, then a night flight.

## The stopovers

**Hong Kong (Cathay)**
- Spanish and Australian passports visa-free; immigration 30–45 min; Airport Express 24 min to town, ~€13 each way.
- Free half-day guided tour for layovers over 7 h (Airport Authority + Trip.com; book up to 3 months ahead; check it still runs in December).
- Night stop: Regal Airport Hotel is attached to the terminal; or a Kowloon hotel (€120–180) — Symphony of Lights 20:00, Temple Street market, dim sum and Star Ferry in the morning. Back at the airport by 12:30 for the 15:20 flight.
- Weather 14–20 °C, dry. Economy fares include no lounge; Plaza Premium sleep rooms ~US$80–120.

**Guangzhou (China Southern)**
- Spain and Australia are on China's **240-hour visa-free transit** list (onward ticket to a third country; passport valid 3+ months). Staying airside needs nothing.
- Through-checked bags; all China Southern flights in Terminal 2; free rest zone with recliners 21:00–08:00; Aerotel hourly rooms airside.
- China Southern gives a **free transit hotel** to eligible long-layover passengers (booked on csair.com with the ticket number; may not apply to multi-city tickets — check before relying on it). Otherwise an airport hotel ~€60–100.
- Weather 12–22 °C, dry. City: Canton Tower, Shamian Island, Cantonese food; metro from the airport ~45 min.

**Bangkok (THAI)**
- Visa-exempt from 15 Sep 2026 (30 days, TDAC form required). Airport Rail Link ~30 min, under €1.20.
- 18 h: Wat Pho and Wat Arun by river boat, Jim Thompson House, Chinatown for dinner; day room or lounge pass for a nap. Miracle Transit Hotel airside ~€77 / 6 h.
- Weather 20–31 °C, dry season.

## Return ticket: book together or separately?

- Round trip **from Perth** on Cathay: €2,780–3,006 for two, **€160–509 cheaper** than two one-ways — but Cathay flies out of Perth only on certain days (none on 7, 14, 15 or 16 Feb; yes on 10, 12, 13 Feb).
- Open-jaw (home from **Sydney or Brisbane**) on one Cathay ticket: €3,214, only €92–125 cheaper than two one-ways; Melbourne had no one-ticket fare.
- One-way home 14 Feb, cheapest without the Middle East: Sydney €1,451, Melbourne €1,485, Brisbane €1,483 for two (Cathay).
- A single ticket fixes the return date now. Our take: book the outbound one-way, buy the east-coast return later.
