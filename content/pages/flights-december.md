## Jet lag: how to arrive with a holiday, not a hangover

Perth is seven hours ahead of Spain. Hong Kong is on Perth time; Bangkok is one hour behind it. The pattern that works is: **sleep on the overnight flight out of Europe, spend a night in the hub on Perth time, then take a daytime flight that lands in Perth in the evening and go straight to bed.** The pattern to avoid is a daytime flight out, a day awake (or asleep) in the hub, and a night flight landing at breakfast — that is how the first day in Perth disappears.

- **Pick A (14 Dec, 22:30):** overnight flight (sleep), land Hong Kong 18:00 Tuesday, dinner and a night in a bed, a morning in the city, 15:20 flight, land Perth 22:55 Wednesday. Best of the lot. ★★★
- **Pick B (13 Dec multi-city):** daytime flight out (less sleep), land Hong Kong 06:50 Monday, a full day plus a proper night, then the same afternoon flight, land 22:55 Tuesday. The extra day in Hong Kong is what resets the clock. ★★★
- **Cathay 13 Dec, 8.5 h connection:** land Hong Kong 06:50 after a flight that felt like an evening, a rushed day trip, land Perth 22:55 having been up for most of 30 hours. Fine, but expect a slow first day. ★★
- **THAI with the 18 h Bangkok day (13 or 15 Dec):** land Bangkok 05:40 after a daytime flight, a full day, then a six-hour night flight landing 07:55. Great day in Bangkok, rough arrival; the first Perth day is for sleeping. A day room in Bangkok softens it. ★★
- **THAI 14 Dec, 33 h Bangkok:** night flight out, a night and a day in Bangkok, then a night flight in. Better than the 18 h version, still lands at breakfast. ★★
- **China Southern:** 28 h with a short connection, lands 21:35 — a decent arrival time, but the least comfortable seats. ★★

## The stopovers

### Hong Kong (Cathay)

- **Entry:** Spanish and Australian passports are both visa-free (90 days), no arrival card. Immigration at a quiet hour is 30–45 minutes.
- **Into town:** Airport Express, 24 minutes to Hong Kong station, about €13 each way (Octopus card); a taxi to Central is about €40. Door to door allow 1.5 hours each way.
- **Free layover tour:** stays of over 7 hours qualify for a free half-day guided tour (Airport Authority + Trip.com), bookable up to three months ahead, fast-track immigration included; routes seen so far are Wong Tai Sin/Sham Shui Po at midday and West Kowloon/Avenue of Stars in the evening. Check it still runs in December.
- **Weather:** 14–20 °C, dry, about four rainy days in the month. The best month to walk Hong Kong.
- **Sleep:** Regal Airport Hotel is landside, connected to the terminal (day rooms and nights). Plaza Premium lounges have private sleeping rooms with showers, open 24 h, roughly US$80–120 for 3–6 hours — book ahead, there are few. Economy fares include no lounge access. A mid-range hotel in Kowloon or Central runs about €120–180 a night.

**21 h night stop (Pick A, 18:00 → 15:20):** Airport Express to Kowloon, check into a Tsim Sha Tsui hotel, walk the harbourfront for the 20:00 Symphony of Lights, dinner at Temple Street night market. Morning: dim sum, Star Ferry across to Central and the Mid-Levels escalator, or Sham Shui Po for the street markets. Train back by 12:30, gate by 14:40. Budget about €200–260 for two including the hotel. (Or do it lazily: sleep at the Regal and take the morning train in.)

**Full day + night (Pick B, 06:50 Monday → 15:20 Tuesday):** everything above plus the Peak Tram, or a Lantau day (Big Buddha / Ngong Ping cable car) — and the free evening tour fits this window.

**8.5 h day connection (06:50 → 15:20):** doable but tight — through immigration by 07:45, train in, Star Ferry and dim sum, on the 12:30 train back. Cabin bag only, and skip the free tour (its midday slot clashes).

### Bangkok (THAI)

- **Entry:** from 15 September 2026 Spain and Australia are visa-exempt for 30 days (tourism) and the Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) is required; check with THAI whether a layover day counts differently. Immigration ranges from 10 minutes to over an hour; a fast-track service (around 06:00–09:00 arrivals) cuts it to 5–10 minutes.
- **Into town:** Airport Rail Link from level B1, 05:30–24:00, about 30 minutes to Phaya Thai for under €1.20, connecting to the BTS Skytrain. Allow six hours as the minimum to leave the airport; eight is comfortable; be back two hours before departure.
- **Weather:** 20–31 °C, dry season, about a 7 % chance of rain on any day. The best time of year to be there.
- **Sleep and showers:** Miracle Transit Hotel is *airside* (Concourse G, floor 4): a six-hour block from about €77, 24 h, only for passengers in transit who have not entered Thailand. Coral and Miracle lounges sell walk-in entry from about THB 1,200 (~€31) with showers. The Hyatt Regency at the airport does flexible 24 h stays with a pool (landside). Magic Food Point, a 24 h Thai food court, is on floor 1.

**18 h day (05:40 → ~midnight):** through immigration by 07:00, ARL to Phaya Thai, Jim Thompson House when it opens, a river boat to Wat Pho and Wat Arun, street-food lunch, a nap in a day room or a riverside hotel pool mid-afternoon, Yaowarat (Chinatown) for dinner and its night market, ARL back by 21:30 (taxi as backup — do not rely on the last train for a midnight flight). About €60–90 for two without a hotel, €150–250 with a day room. Worth leaving the airport for; the 2 h and 9 h connections are not.

**33 h (night + day):** book a city hotel for the night (€50–80) and spread the same sights over two half-days, with more time at Wat Pho. Leave the city by mid-morning for an afternoon flight, or after dinner for the night flight.

### Singapore (for comparison only)

No Singapore Airlines fare showed up from London, Madrid or Barcelona on our dates, so this is a reference point: Changi offers a free 2.5 h Singapore tour for layovers of 5.5–24 h (bookable 50 days ahead, be back 90 minutes before departure), a free Jewel tour (Rain Vortex, Canopy Park; the evening one includes the light show), and the Aerotel transit hotel's pool with a day pass of about €13 including jacuzzi and showers. It is the easiest airport to enjoy without leaving — but it is not on the table this time.

## Verdict

**Book Pick A — Cathay from Madrid on Monday 14 December at 22:30 with the 21-hour Hong Kong night, €1,893 for two (€947 each), landing Perth Wednesday 16 December at 22:55.** It is the latest comfortable departure that stays under €1,000 each, it is one ticket from Spain, the Hong Kong night costs nothing extra, and it is the itinerary most likely to deliver us to Perth ready for the first day.

If a full day in Hong Kong sounds better than €130, **Pick B** (Sunday 13 December, multi-city to the 15th, €1,763) does that and lands a day earlier. If the budget wins, **THAI from Milan on the 13th at €1,484** is the honest cheapest, with a great Bangkok day and a tired first morning in Perth.

Before booking: confirm the Cathay through-fare really is the same price with the 21 h connection on the day, check whether bags can be short-checked to Hong Kong, book the Hong Kong hotel the same evening, and check the free layover tour slots.
