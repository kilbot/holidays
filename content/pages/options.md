# Adeline's adventures

- **What this is:** each thing Adeline asked for in [her plan](/scenarios/adeline-west-to-east), researched into an option. None of them is in a scenario yet.
- **Researched:** 1 Oct 2026, from park, tourism and operator sites first. "Not found" means no source gave it.
- **Costs:** for two, in AUD, with EUR at the site's rate (A$1 = €0.61). Fuel is extra unless stated.
- **Dates:** "Fits the route" uses Scenario 1's weeks.

## Western Australia

### Surf lesson, Margaret River

- **Where:** Prevelly / Gnarabup and Yallingup, near the Margaret River base.
- **What exactly:** a 2-hour beginner group lesson with board and wetsuit. Margaret River Surfing Academy from A$50 each; Margaret River Surf School A$39–43 each on resellers. Yallingup Surf School also teaches; no price found.
- **How long:** half a day.
- **Dec–Feb:** mean max 26–27 °C in Jan–Feb (weather2travel, not BoM).
- **Fits the route:** Margaret River base, 16–22 Dec.
- **Cost for two:** A$78–100 (€48–61).
- **Book ahead:** online before you land; school holidays start mid-December.
- **Caveat:** the same lesson is listed at A$32–50 depending on the platform. Check the price at checkout.
- **Sources:** [Margaret River Surfing Academy (Rezdy)](https://mrsurf.rezdy.com/760818/family-group-surf-lesson-pass-margaret-river-surfing-academy) · [Yallingup Surf School, margaretriver.com](https://www.margaretriver.com/?p=1850972) · [weather2travel, Margaret River](https://weather2travel.com/western-australia/margaret-river/february/)

### Organic and biodynamic cellar doors, Margaret River

- **Where:** Margaret River wine region, around the base.
- **What exactly:** four cellar doors:
  - Cullen Wines: biodynamic and organic since 2003, seated tastings from A$15 each, waived if you buy.
  - Voyager Estate: certified organic (2023); tasting fee not found.
  - Burnside Organic Farm: organic and biodynamic, orange wine and pét-nat; fee not found.
  - Marri Wood Park: Demeter biodynamic; free tasting.
- **How long:** 4–5 h for three or four stops.
- **Dec–Feb:** opening over Christmas and Boxing Day not found; check each.
- **Fits the route:** Margaret River base, 16–22 Dec.
- **Cost for two:** about A$30 (€18) at Cullen, free at Marri Wood Park, others not found.
- **Book ahead:** book the Cullen and Voyager seated tastings ahead in peak season.
- **Caveat:** one of you drives and doesn't drink; random breath testing is strict.
- **Sources:** [margaretriver.com, organic and biodynamic wine](https://www.margaretriver.com/?p=1854386) · [Naturaliste Charters, best wineries](https://naturalistecharters.com.au/blogs/best-wineries-margaret-river-2/)

### Cape to Cape Track, day sections

- **Where:** Leeuwin-Naturaliste National Park, along the coast from the base.
- **What exactly:** the best one-day sections:
  - Redgate Beach → Contos → Boranup karri forest: about 14 km.
  - Prevelly → Ellensbrook: 12 km, about 4 h.
  - Yallingup → Cape Naturaliste Lighthouse: 14 km, about 4.5 h.
  - Short options: Smiths Point → Injidup 5.8 km; Cape Naturaliste → Sugarloaf Rock 3.4 km, sealed path.
- **How long:** 4–5 h walking, plus a car shuttle or an out-and-back.
- **Dec–Feb:** the park agency advises against the hot months for long walks; start at dawn and check the fire danger rating.
- **Fits the route:** Margaret River base, 16–22 Dec.
- **Cost for two:** no park fee found on the DBCA track page.
- **Book ahead:** no booking.
- **Caveat:** total fire ban days can close sections. Check DBCA park alerts that morning.
- **Sources:** [DBCA Explore Parks, Cape to Cape Track](https://exploreparks.dbca.wa.gov.au/trail/cape-cape-track) · [We Are Explorers, Cape to Cape](https://weareexplorers.co/cape-to-cape-track-western-australia/)

- **Where:** Fremantle, 30 min by train from Perth.
- **What exactly:**

- **How long:** 2–3 h in the park, then the beach.
- **Dec–Feb:** Perth mean max about 28 °C in Dec, 29 °C in Jan; sea about 19–20 °C in Dec.

- **Book ahead:** no booking.
- **Caveat:** check the Beachsafe app for patrols and conditions; the sea breeze can blow up in the afternoon.

## Sydney

### New Year's Eve fireworks spot

- **Where:** Sydney Harbour foreshore.
- **What exactly:**
  - Free, first come: Mrs Macquarie's Point, Barangaroo Reserve, Observatory Hill, Dawes Point, Bennelong Lawn and others.
  - Free with a ticket or ballot: Opera House Forecourt (tickets from 26 Dec, 10:00), Cahill Expressway (ballot).
  - Paid: Taronga Zoo from A$125, Royal Botanic Garden picnic A$315 a guest; Lower Bradfield Park on sale 7 Oct.
- **How long:** free spots take the whole day.
- **Dec–Feb:** Thu 31 Dec. Sydney January mean max about 28 °C (BoM, Jan 2026).
- **Fits the route:** the Sydney week, 30 Dec – 5 Jan.
- **Cost for two:** free, or A$250 (€153) at Taronga, or A$630 (€384) at the Botanic Garden picnic. Public transport is capped at A$9.65 each a day on Fri–Sun.
- **Book ahead:** now. Several paid spots and the island spots are already sold out.
- **Caveat:** in 2023 Mrs Macquarie's Point was full before the gates opened at 11:00, and people queue from 30 Dec. Several sites are alcohol-free.
- **Sources:** [Sydney NYE, ticketed vantage points](https://www.sydneynewyearseve.com/ticketed-vantage-points) · [Sydney.com, New Year's Eve fireworks](https://www.sydney.com/destinations/sydney/sydney-city/sydney-harbour/new-years-eve-fireworks)

### Bondi to Coogee coastal walk

- **Where:** Sydney's eastern beaches.
- **What exactly:** 6 km one way past Bondi, Tamarama, Bronte, Clovelly and Coogee; ocean pools at Bondi Icebergs, Bronte, Wylies Baths and Giles Baths.
- **How long:** 2–3 h walking; a day with swims. Bus back from Coogee.
- **Dec–Feb:** about 12 rain days in January; exposed, so hat and water (fountains at every beach).
- **Fits the route:** the Sydney week, 1–5 Jan.
- **Cost for two:** free, plus about A$19 (€12) of buses.
- **Book ahead:** no booking.
- **Caveat:** very crowded in holiday week. Swim between the flags.
- **Sources:** [Sydney.com, Bondi to Coogee walk](https://www.sydney.com/things-to-do/nature-and-parks/walks/bondi-to-coogee-coastal-walk) · [BoM, Sydney January 2026](https://www.bom.gov.au/climate/current/month/nsw/archive/202601.sydney.shtml)

### Blue Mountains day trip

- **Where:** Katoomba and Leura, about 2 h by train from Sydney Central.
- **What exactly:** Echo Point for the Three Sisters, then the Prince Henry Cliff Walk: 7 km one way, 3–4 h, Katoomba Cascades to Gordon Falls, ending in Leura village. Shorter sections join from Cliff Drive.
- **How long:** a full day, about 4 h of it on the train.
- **Dec–Feb:** the parks service closes tracks for fire danger and bad weather.
- **Fits the route:** the Sydney week, a weekday: Mon 4 or Tue 5 Jan.
- **Cost for two:** train about A$27–38 return for two (€16–23); walking is free.
- **Book ahead:** no booking; trains about hourly.
- **Caveat:** a total fire ban can close tracks. Check NSW National Parks alerts the evening before.
- **Sources:** [NSW National Parks, Prince Henry Cliff Walk](https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/things-to-do/walking-tracks/prince-henry-cliff-walk)

### Jervis Bay: Hyams Beach and Booderee

- **Where:** NSW South Coast, about 224 km and 2 h 45 min south of Sydney.
- **What exactly:** Hyams Beach's white sand, then Booderee National Park (Green Patch, Hole in the Wall), 10 min apart.
- **How long:** a long day trip at best; one night is better.
- **Dec–Feb:** peak season. Hyams has about 450 parking spaces against up to 4,000 cars on peak days, and the village closes to cars once it is full (Boxing Day to Australia Day).
- **Fits the route:** needs a hire car from Sydney; the Sydney week, 2–5 Jan.
- **Cost for two:** Booderee A$20 (€12) a car for the day; car hire and fuel extra.
- **Book ahead:** accommodation well ahead for January.
- **Caveat:** on busy days you may not get into Hyams at all.
- **Sources:** [Booderee National Park, FAQ](https://booderee.gov.au/faq) · [Ulladulla Times, Hyams Beach parking](https://www.ulladullatimes.com.au/story/9277722/shoalhaven-council-targets-hyams-beach-with-paid-parking-plans/)

## Tasmania

### kunanyi / Mount Wellington

- **Where:** above Hobart, in Wellington Park.
- **What exactly:** drive Pinnacle Road to the summit, or take the Explorer Bus from the Hobart visitor centre (2 h return, 30 min at the top). Walk the Organ Pipes: about 3 h return, graded difficult.
- **How long:** half a day.
- **Dec–Feb:** the summit is far colder than Hobart and it can snow in any month; Pinnacle Road closes in snow.
- **Fits the route:** a clear day from the Huon base, 6–19 Jan.
- **Cost for two:** free by car; bus fare not found.
- **Book ahead:** bus online the day before.
- **Caveat:** exposed on top. Take warm layers and a rain jacket even in January.
- **Sources:** [Discover Tasmania, kunanyi Explorer Bus](https://www.discovertasmania.com.au/things-to-do/tours/kunanyimtwellingtonexplorerbus)

### Freycinet and Wineglass Bay

- **Where:** Freycinet National Park, Coles Bay; about 2.5 h from Hobart.
- **What exactly:** Wineglass Bay Lookout, 2.6 km return, 1–1.5 h; or the Wineglass Bay and Hazards Beach circuit, 11 km, 4–5 h.
- **How long:** a full day; two nights on the east coast saves long drives.
- **Dec–Feb:** January about 23 °C high, 16 °C low.
- **Fits the route:** the road trip, 20 Jan – 2 Feb, first stop.
- **Cost for two:** Holiday Pass A$95.50 (€58) a car, every national park for 2 months, Cradle Mountain included.
- **Book ahead:** Coles Bay beds well ahead. Camping at Wineglass Bay is a ballot drawn on 1 Aug, so it is gone.
- **Caveat:** the lookout track is busy at midday. Walk early or late.
- **Sources:** [Parks and Wildlife Tasmania, Wineglass Bay and Hazards Beach](https://parks.tas.gov.au/explore-our-parks/freycinet-national-park/wineglass-bay-and-hazards-beach-circuit) · [Parks and Wildlife Tasmania, passes and fees](https://parks.tas.gov.au/explore-our-parks/know-before-you-go/park-passes-and-entry-fees)

### Bay of Fires and Binalong Bay

- **Where:** north-east coast, near St Helens.
- **What exactly:** Binalong Bay beaches and The Gardens, the orange-lichen rocks. Eight free campgrounds along the coast (Cosy Corner, Policemans Point); no permit needed.
- **How long:** one night camping, or a long day.
- **Dec–Feb:** peak season; camps fill.
- **Fits the route:** the road trip, between Freycinet and Launceston.
- **Cost for two:** camping free.
- **Book ahead:** no booking; arrive by midday for a site.
- **Caveat:** no water or firewood in the southern section. Bring your own. Pit toilets only.
- **Sources:** [Parks and Wildlife Tasmania, Bay of Fires camping](https://parks.tas.gov.au/explore-our-parks/bay-of-fires-conservation-area/bay-of-fires-camping)

### Cradle Mountain, 2–3 days of day walks

- **Where:** Cradle Mountain–Lake St Clair National Park, north-west.
- **What exactly:**
  - Dove Lake circuit: 6 km, 2–3 h, easy.
  - Marions Lookout from Ronny Creek: about 7 km return, 2.5–3 h, a steep chained section.
  - Cradle Mountain summit: 12 km return, 6–8 h, a scramble to 1,545 m.
  - Shuttle bus from the visitor centre every 15 min.
- **How long:** day 1 Dove Lake and Marions; day 2 the summit; day 3 for bad weather.
- **Dec–Feb:** January about 20 °C high; the weather turns fast.
- **Fits the route:** the road trip, 20 Jan – 2 Feb, after Launceston.
- **Cost for two:** covered by the Holiday Pass (Freycinet card); without it A$59.60 (€36) a day for two, shuttle included. Sources differ on whether the Holiday Pass covers the shuttle.
- **Book ahead:** book beds as soon as dates are set (Discovery Parks cabins and sites; Waldheim Cabins, 2-night minimum).
- **Caveat:** the summit scramble is unsafe in cloud and wind.
- **Sources:** [Parks and Wildlife Tasmania, passes and fees](https://parks.tas.gov.au/explore-our-parks/know-before-you-go/park-passes-and-entry-fees) · [Discover Tasmania, Waldheim Cabins](https://www.discovertasmania.com.au/accommodation/waldheimcabins/)

### Cape Byron walk and lighthouse

- **Where:** Walgun Cape Byron State Conservation Area, about 15–18 min from Brunswick Heads or Mullumbimby.
- **What exactly:** a 3.7 km loop with steps and short steep hills, from Captain Cook Lookout, The Pass, Wategos or the lighthouse.
- **How long:** 1–3 h.
- **Dec–Feb:** hot and humid; no humpbacks (they pass in winter).
- **Fits the route:** Northern Rivers, 3–12 Feb, an early morning.
- **Cost for two:** free walk; parking A$10 an hour at the lighthouse (1 h max), A$5 an hour at The Pass.
- **Book ahead:** no booking.
- **Caveat:** parking is scarce. Go early or start from Wategos on foot.
- **Sources:** [NSW National Parks, Walgun Cape Byron walking track](https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/things-to-do/walking-tracks/walgun-cape-byron-walking-track/visitor-info)

### Surf around Byron

- **Where:** The Pass and Wategos (Byron Bay); Broken Head and Lennox Head for the experienced.
- **What exactly:** Wategos is a slow right-hander for beginners; The Pass is a long right point for all levels. Lessons: Soul Surf School A$59 each (4 h, gear included); Byron Bay Surf School A$60 each (2.5 h).
- **How long:** a morning.
- **Dec–Feb:** summer is often small at The Pass (its best season is Apr–Sep).
- **Fits the route:** Northern Rivers, 3–12 Feb, early in the stay.
- **Cost for two:** A$118–120 (€72–73) for a lesson; parking A$5–10 an hour at The Pass.
- **Book ahead:** Soul Surf School takes reservations; book once dates are set.
- **Caveat:** the points are crowded and the locals expect etiquette; rocky entries at Broken Head and Lennox.
- **Sources:** [NSW National Parks, Soul Surf School](https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/things-to-do/guided-tours/soul-surf-school-lessons-at-byron-bay) · [Elements of Byron, surf breaks](https://elementsofbyron.com.au/things-to-do/journal/byrons-best-surf-beaches-and-breaks)

### Nimbin day

- **Where:** Nimbin, about 1 h from Mullumbimby; Protesters Falls near The Channon on the way.
- **What exactly:** the main street (Nimbin Museum, Hemp Embassy, murals), the 750 m Rainbow Walk, then Protesters Falls in Nightcap National Park: 1.4 km return, about 45 min.
- **How long:** 5–7 h with the driving.
- **Dec–Feb:** Nightcap can close for bad weather or fire danger.
- **Fits the route:** Northern Rivers, 3–12 Feb, any day.
- **Cost for two:** gold-coin donation at the Hemp Museum; the falls are free.
- **Book ahead:** no booking.
- **Caveat:** the town is built around cannabis culture; that is the point and also the catch.
- **Sources:** [Visit NSW, The Channon, Protesters Falls and Nimbin](https://visitnsw.com/destinations/north-coast/lismore-area/lismore/journey/the-channon-protesters-falls-nimbin-and-mount-nardi) · [NSW National Parks, Nightcap National Park](https://www.nationalparks.nsw.gov.au/visit-a-park/parks/nightcap-national-park)

### Rainforest walk: Minyon Falls

- **Where:** Nightcap National Park, World Heritage rainforest, inland from Mullumbimby.
- **What exactly:** Minyon Falls walking track, 13 km return, 3–4 h; the falls drop 100 m, and the Boggy Creek walk nearby has swimming holes.
- **How long:** half a day plus the drive.
- **Dec–Feb:** pick a dry day; the parks service closes tracks in bad weather.
- **Fits the route:** Northern Rivers, 3–12 Feb, a dry midweek day.
- **Cost for two:** park fee not found.
- **Book ahead:** no booking.

### Yamba and Angourie

- **Where:** Clarence Valley, about 127 km (1.5 h) south of Byron.
- **What exactly:** Angourie Point, a National Surfing Reserve (for experienced surfers); the Blue and Green Pools, spring-fed quarries by the sea; Yamba Farmers Market, Wednesdays 7:00–11:00.
- **How long:** a full day, or 2–3 nights as the finish.
- **Dec–Feb:** the pools can close for algae in warm months.
- **Fits the route:** the end of Scenario 1, before flying home; a Wednesday for the market (10 Feb).
- **Cost for two:** the pools are free.
- **Book ahead:** no booking for the pools.
- **Caveat:** Angourie Point is an expert break and some beaches are unpatrolled. Brisbane Airport is about 1 h 50 from Byron; Ballina's flights go to Sydney and Melbourne.
