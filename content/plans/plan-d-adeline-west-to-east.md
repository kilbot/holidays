# Australia — 2 Months, West to East

A 2-month itinerary for two, mid-December to late February. Built around family time in Perth, New Year's Eve in Sydney, bohemian small towns, nature, surf, markets and hiking, with 6 weeks of remote work from fixed bases and 2 weeks of proper holidays.

---

## 🍇 Week 1 — Margaret River: Work + Surf + Wineries

**Arrive in Perth mid-December. Drive 3 hours south. Rent a car from Perth.**

Base: an Airbnb in or around **Margaret River**, with reliable Wi-Fi. Start with the west while you're out here, before heading back to Perth for Christmas.

This region is the west coast at its best:

- 🏄 World-class surf (Surfers Point, Yallingup)
- 🍷 Wineries and local producers, many organic
- 🌳 Karri forests and coastal walks (Cape to Cape Track day sections)
- ☕ Small artisan towns: Margaret River, Cowaramup, Yallingup, Augusta
- 🧺 Weekend farmers markets

Work during the week, explore evenings and weekends. December weather in WA is hot and dry, perfect for late-afternoon beach sessions.

---

## 🎄 Week 2 — Perth: Christmas with Family

**Drive back to Perth (3 hours).**

Christmas with the family. This week is for family, shaking off any leftover jetlag, and easing off. Light work at most.

Worth doing around Perth:

- A day in **Fremantle**: markets, street art, historic port, craft breweries, artist community
- Cottesloe Beach at sunset
- Kings Park and the botanic garden
- Mount Lawley / Northbridge for the café, bar and live-music scene

---

## 🎆 Week 3 — Sydney: New Year's Eve

**✈️ Flight 1: Perth → Sydney (~5h), around 30 December.**

New Year's Eve in Sydney, as wanted. Fireworks over the Harbour, one of the most spectacular displays in the world. Book a viewing spot early: the best vantage points go months ahead, and many are paid or ticketed.

The rest of the week in Sydney:

- **Newtown / Enmore** (Inner West): the alternative heart of the city. Thrift stores, record shops, veggie cafés, live-music venues, street art. The perfect combo for the two of you.
- **Marrickville** market on Sunday (organic, seasonal)
- Bondi to Coogee coastal walk
- **Blue Mountains** as a day trip (1h30): Katoomba, a bohemian mountain village, nature
- Jervis Bay to the south if time allows: some of the whitest sand in the world

---

## 🌿 Weeks 4–5 — Tasmania: Huon Valley

**✈️ Flight 2: Sydney → Hobart (direct, ~2h). Rent a car on arrival.**

Base: an Airbnb in **Cygnet or Franklin**, south of Hobart, for two weeks.

Cygnet is exactly the vibe of this trip: artists, local producers, markets, farms, cafés, rural atmosphere. The Huon Valley is orchard and cider country with beautiful landscapes in every direction.

While working:

- Quiet life in a little house, shopping at local markets
- Hikes after work
- Weekend trips: Hobart (Salamanca Market, Battery Point, kunanyi/Mount Wellington, MONA), Bruny Island

Big advantage of the late-January timing: Australian school holidays are over, so fewer crowds and better prices than at Christmas.

---

## 🏔️ Weeks 6–7 — Tasmania Road Trip (Proper Holidays)

These are the two weeks off work. February is objectively the best hiking month in Tasmania: the most stable weather of the year and clear trails.

### Week 6: East Coast

**Cygnet → Freycinet → Binalong Bay / Bay of Fires → Launceston**

- Wineglass Bay and Freycinet National Park
- Wild beaches
- Bay of Fires
- Small coastal towns, local producers, vineyards
- Hiking

### Week 7: Mountains + North-West

**Launceston → Cradle Mountain → Stanley / North-West Coast → Hobart**

The big highlight: **Cradle Mountain**, 2–3 days. Day hikes rather than the full Overland Track keeps it flexible.

Keep **one completely unplanned day**: just drive, hike, stop in a little town, have lunch somewhere random and see where the day takes you.

> Book Cradle Mountain accommodation early. It fills up even in February.

---

## 🌴 Weeks 8–9 — Northern Rivers: The Hippie Side

**✈️ Flight 3: Hobart → Ballina. Rent a car.**

### Base: Brunswick Heads or Mullumbimby (1 week, working)

Skip staying in Byron itself, which is touristy nowadays. The hinterland is where the real bohemian culture lives:

- Brunswick Heads, Mullumbimby, Bangalow
- Byron Bay and Cape Byron for day visits
- Lennox Head, Broken Head
- Local markets everywhere
- Surf spots and rainforest hikes
- A day in **Nimbin** for the atmosphere and the area's alternative history

Late February heads-up: it is still the humid season up there. Warm ocean, good surf, but expect muggy days and the odd storm. The strong El Niño shaping up for summer 2026–27 tilts the odds toward a slightly drier-than-average Wet, so your rain odds are a bit better than usual, though it won't change the muggy feel. Check the BoM three-month outlook before you leave.

### Finish: Yamba / Angourie (2–3 nights)

Smaller, quieter and more focused on surf, nature and local community than Byron. Angourie is a legendary surf spot; Yamba has a proper farmers market.

The perfect final day:

> Coffee in the morning → surf → local market → walk → sunset → little restaurant → back to the Airbnb.

**Finish: Ballina/Yamba → Brisbane** (about 2h30 by road, or a short flight from Ballina). **✈️ Fly home from Brisbane.**

## ✈️ Flights & Cars

**Three internal flights**: Perth → Sydney, Sydney → Hobart, Hobart → Ballina. Plus the final leg Ballina/Yamba → Brisbane for the flight home. Everything else is by car, rented only where it adds value:

- Perth / Margaret River (week 1)
- Tasmania (full stay)
- Northern Rivers

## 💰 Budget

Revised up from the first estimate, because of the extra transcontinental Perth–Sydney flight and arriving in peak season. Budget more like **€12,500–15,000 for two, flights included**, bearing in mind:

- Europe → Perth flights between 10 and 20 December are expensive (peak season), €1,200–1,800 per person depending on the date
- Three internal flights instead of two
- Accommodation is the biggest variable; four fixed 1–2 week Airbnb bases keep it under control

Treat this range as a realistic baseline rather than an optimistic floor. Refine it once you have real flight prices.

Booking priorities, in order:

1. Europe → Perth flights (mid-December = peak, book ASAP)
2. Perth → Sydney flight + Sydney accommodation for NYE week (goes very fast)
3. Perth accommodation for Christmas
4. Cradle Mountain (fills up fast)
5. Everything else can wait a little

## ❤️ Why This Version

Best of both worlds: family and the wild west at the start, New Year's Eve in Sydney as he wanted, Tasmania at its best hiking moment, and the alternative communities of the Northern Rivers to finish. One more flight than the all-west version, but you keep both ends of the country.
