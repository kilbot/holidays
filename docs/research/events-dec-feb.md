# Events layer — what's on, Dec 2026 – Feb 2027

Research for [issue #20](https://github.com/kilbot/holidays/issues/20). Machine-readable twin: [`events-dec-feb.json`](./events-dec-feb.json).

**Researched:** 27 August 2026. All sources accessed that day.
**Currency:** AUD, per couple unless stated. FX A$1 = €0.61.
**Weighting:** per `docs/CONTEXT.md` — `festival` / `market` / `doof` are **first-class sway-events** (hers); `sport` is tagged but **minor** and never justifies a big spend or a relocation.

## The one finding that reframes the whole sweep

**The Australian bush-doof calendar deliberately avoids December–February.** Fire danger, total fire bans and extreme heat push the scene into Sep–Nov and Feb–Apr. This is not an inference — Rainbow Serpent (now **Rainbow Spirit**) said it in writing when it permanently abandoned its Australia Day long-weekend slot:

> "The increased temperatures and fire risk over summer has become a real concern (especially with our lovely site being adjacent to a forest reserve)." — [rainbowspirit.net](https://rainbowspirit.net/new-festival-dates-announced/)

Every named target from the issue lands outside the window as a direct result:

| Target | Status | Why it's out |
|---|---|---|
| **Esoteric** | ☠️ **Ruled out** | 2025 edition cancelled the night before opening (permit refused); company in **voluntary administration owing A$2.8m+**. March event anyway. |
| **Rainbow Serpent / Rainbow Spirit** | ☠️ Moved | Renamed 2023, permanently off summer → Easter / early March, on fire-risk grounds. |
| **Earth Frequency** | ☠️ Moved | Was February at Ivory's Rock; now **23–26 Oct 2026** at Woodfordia. |
| **Subsonic** | ☠️ Misses | **13–15 Nov 2026**, Barrington Tops — before they arrive. |
| **Rabbits Eat Lettuce** | ☠️ Misses | **25–29 Mar 2027** (Easter), Beebo QLD — after departure. |
| **Pitch, Strawberry Fields, Dragon Dreaming, Jungle Love, Meredith** | ☠️ | All Sep–Nov or Mar–May. |
| **Mona Foma** | ☠️ **Defunct** | Cancelled permanently. Any source still listing it is stale. |
| **Falls Festival** | ☠️ **Defunct** | Still appears in lazy tourism copy. Not running. |

**What survives:** a dense **NYE festival cluster** (which fights the Sydney anchor head-on), then near-silence until **Party In The Paddock, 4–7 Feb**. The compensation is **hippie/artisan markets** — free, year-round, and they turn out to be the sharpest date-placement levers in the dataset.

## Grouped by fortnight

### 1–15 Dec 2026 — WA / Perth home base
- **Aus v NZ 1st Test**, Optus Stadium Perth, **9–13 Dec** — `sport` `minor`. Free lodging, borrowed car, GA day ≈ A$80–160/couple. Tag it, don't build on it.
- **Twilight Hawkers Market**, Forrest Place — **every Friday**, 16:30–21:00, free entry.
- **Fremantle Markets** — **every Fri/Sat/Sun**, 09:00–18:00, free entry. The WA hippie-market stand-in.
- **Margaret River Farmers Market** — **Saturdays only**, 07:30–11:30. ⚠️ *The Margaret River capsule must straddle a Saturday or it misses this entirely.*
- ☠️ Meredith Music Festival, 11–13 Dec (VIC, ballot-only) — a near-miss, not an option.

### 16–31 Dec 2026 — Christmas Perth → Sydney NYE
- **Boxing Day, 26 Dec** is triple-booked: **PER→SYD flight** + **Sydney–Hobart start** (free harbour spectacle) + **Opera House Forecourt NYE ticket release at 10am AEDT / 7am Perth**. Don't try to stack all three.
- **🔥 Woodford Folk Festival — 27 Dec – 1 Jan, Woodfordia QLD.** The flagship Australian hippie gathering: 6 days, 2,000+ performers, huge artisan market, workshops, NYE fire event. **On pure taste this is the single best-fitting event of the entire trip for her — and it is a direct collision with the Sydney NYE hard anchor.** Tickets **on sale now** (A$25–745 pp; full festival + camping ≈ **A$1,100–1,500/couple**); program mid-Oct.
- **🔥 Lost Paradise — 28 Dec – 1 Jan, Glenworth Valley NSW (~1h north of Sydney).** Four-day bush camping festival **covering NYE itself**, forest staging, wellness/workshop program. Lineup: John Summit, Skepta, KI/KI, Overmono, Nia Archives, Armand Van Helden. **≈ A$1,250–1,600/couple all-in**, on sale since 5 Aug 2026. **The sharpest anchor conflict in the calendar** — it is the only NYE option that serves her taste, and it is mutually exclusive with Sydney Harbour.
- **Taste of Summer**, Hobart waterfront, 27 Dec – 3 Jan — free entry. Unreachable given the anchor.
- **Great Escape NYE**, Redbanks/Nugent TAS, 29 Dec – 1 Jan — *likely, re-check Sep*. Three stages, BYO, camping, <1h from Hobart.
- Beyond The Valley (VIC, 28 Dec – 1 Jan); NYE On The Hill (VIC, 30 Dec – 1 Jan); Wildlands Brisbane (31 Dec) — all dominated by Lost Paradise for this trip.
- **Byron Community Market** bonus summer edition, **Sun 20 Dec**.

### 1–15 Jan 2027 — post-NYE Sydney tail
- **Wildlands Perth, 2 Jan**, Arena Joondalup — lands **two days after they leave WA**. Unavoidable miss.
- **Field Day**, The Domain Sydney — *TBA, historically 1 Jan*. The one electronic event **compatible** with the anchor rather than competing with it. Re-check Oct.
- **New Year's Test**, SCG, **4–8 Jan** — `sport` `minor`. Day 1 falls on the last day of the Sydney capsule's ideal 6-night shape; no schedule change needed.
- **Sydney Festival, 7–25 Jan** — large free program (Domain concerts, outdoor installations).
- **🔥 Cygnet Folk Festival — 8–10 Jan, Huon Valley TAS.** A genuinely hippie village folk festival — buskers, markets, workshops, whole-town takeover. **Tickets on sale now** (≈ A$400/couple, est.). Strong taste fit, wrong month: it sits a month before the planned February Tasmania block and on top of the reef window.
- **🔥 The Channon Craft Market — Sun 10 Jan.** *See the placement problem below.*

### 16–31 Jan 2027 — reef / Australia Day / Byron
- **Australian Open, 11–31 Jan**, Melbourne Park. `sport` `minor`. **Re-ranked down**: a cheap ground-pass afternoon (from A$49 pp) if already in Melbourne, never a reason to fly there. Also inflates Melbourne lodging all month.
- **Midsumma Festival, 17 Jan – 7 Feb**, Melbourne — three weeks of queer arts running straight through Australia Day. **A better argument for late-January Melbourne than the tennis is.** **Pride March Sun 31 Jan**, free, St Kilda.
- **Byron Community Market** bonus summer edition, **Sun 17 Jan**; Uki Buttery Bazaar + Federal Village Market also **Sun 17 Jan**.
- Tamworth Country Music Festival, 15–24 Jan — off-route, but it is why inland-NSW lodging is scarce mid-January.
- FRINGE WORLD Perth, **20 Jan – 14 Feb** — WA's biggest event, 3½ weeks after they leave. *Structural note: the WA block is scheduled a month before WA's festival season begins.*
- **🔥 Bangalow Market — Sun 24 Jan.** *See below.*
- **Nimbin Markets — Sun 24 Jan AND Sun 31 Jan** (4th + 5th Sundays).
- **🔴 AUSTRALIA DAY — Tue 26 Jan.** Full re-rank in the next section.
- **Frequency Forest, January edition — RUMOUR.** Ran 23–26 Jan 2026 (Australia Day weekend) at Maitland NSW; two stages of techno/hard dance/psy-tech/**progressive psytrance**, camping. Only the Sep 2026 edition is announced. *If a Jan 2027 edition appears it is the only unblocked psytrance camping festival in the window.* **Re-check Oct 2026.**
- **Mullumbimby Farmers Market — Fri 29 Jan**; **Byron Artisan Market — Sat 30 Jan** (evening, Railway Park). Both fall inside the proposed Byron block for free.

### 1–14 Feb 2027 — Tasmania / departure tail
- **🔥🔥 PARTY IN THE PADDOCK — 4–7 Feb, Quercus Park, Carrick TAS** (15 min from Launceston). **The headline sway-event of the window.** Four-day camping music festival, 14th year, returning after a record-breaking 2026 — and **the only in-window camping festival that does not fight a hard anchor.** ≈ A$700–1,000/couple (est.). **NOT YET ON SALE — lineup ~Oct, tickets ~early Nov. Highest booking urgency in this document.**
- **🔥 Australian Wooden Boat Festival — 5–8 Feb, Hobart waterfront.** Free entry, **biennial** (missing it costs two years). Parade of Sail Fri 5 Feb midday; Admiral's Sail Mon 8 Feb 16:00.
- **Royal Hobart Regatta — 6–8 Feb**, Derwent. Free. **Mon 8 Feb is a southern-Tasmania public holiday** — closures and holiday pricing.
- **🔥 Salamanca Market — every Saturday.** **Sat 6 Feb stacks Salamanca + Wooden Boat Festival + Regatta in one walkable day** — the best-value day in the February half of the trip.
- ⚠️ **Tasmania fork:** Party In The Paddock (north, 4–7 Feb) and the Wooden Boat Festival (Hobart, 5–8 Feb) overlap and sit 200 km apart. The Tasmania capsule **cannot have both at full strength**. Choose.
- **Byron Community Market — Sun 7 Feb.** Sydney **Lunar New Year** from Sat 6 Feb (free).
- **Laneway Festival — Sydney Fri 12 Feb / Brisbane Sat 13 Feb** (Melbourne 19 Feb). If departure slips to mid-February, Brisbane on 13 Feb is a 2-hour drive from Byron and a strong last-night option. Adelaide and Perth are sitting 2027 out.
- **Summer Campout, 12–14 Feb — RUMOUR.** The only doof-scene listing anywhere in the window beyond the NYE cluster. Dates but **no state, venue, lineup or ticket link**. Re-check Nov.
- **Channon Craft Market — Sun 14 Feb.**

## 🔴 Australia Day 2027 — re-ranked, both-of-them lens

**Tue 26 January 2027. No long weekend** — which also means no Australia Day long-weekend doof, the slot Rainbow Serpent used to own.

### What changed

The prior recommendation in `capsule-sydney-nye.md` was **Melbourne, because AO quarter/semi-final week + the scheduled BBL final**. Under the taste profile that argument collapses:

- The **AO** is `sport` `minor` — a A$49 ground pass, not a reason to fly.
- The **BBL final** is scheduled for 26 Jan but its **venue is unknown until after 17 January** and could be any of eight cities. Planning a day around a fixture whose city is unknown nine days out was always fragile; now it's also one-sided.

**Melbourne still wins on the day — but for a completely different reason.**

### The re-rank

| # | Where | What 26 Jan actually is, for *both* of them | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sydney** | **Yabun Festival**, Victoria Park Camperdown — free, Aboriginal-owned and led, 65,000+ people, and structurally a **music-and-market day**: live stages, market stalls, dance, panels. Plus WugulOra dawn ceremony at Barangaroo, Harbourfest, the Ferrython (free tickets, limited, released in January) and 9pm fireworks. | **Best program in the country, still the wrong geography.** Backtracks against an east/north flow and re-runs a harbour they already saw at maximum on 31 Dec. Unchanged conclusion — but the reason is now Yabun, not fireworks. |
| 2 | **Melbourne** ⭐ | **Share the Spirit Festival**, Treasury Gardens, **12:00–18:30, free** — Victoria's longest-running First Peoples festival (Songlines Aboriginal Music Corporation): Indigenous folk, country, rock, blues, soul, funk, hip hop; family-friendly, alcohol-free. **That is a real music festival for her, on the day, for nothing.** Plus a free Fed Square program 10:00–19:00, and **Midsumma running 17 Jan – 7 Feb** around it. The tennis is a footnote. | **Still the recommendation if the flow puts them there** — on new grounds. The case for *flying in specially* is much weaker than it was. |
| 3 | **Byron hinterland** 🆕 | New entrant, and on taste arguably the best fit. The day itself is low-key in the Northern Rivers, but it is **bracketed by markets**: **Bangalow Market Sun 24 Jan** and **Nimbin Markets Sun 31 Jan**, with the region's counter-observance culture the most congenial in the country to her. | **The quiet winner if the flow allows it.** See the placement note below. |
| 4 | **Cairns / Port Douglas** | Free Esplanade afternoon/evening ~12:00–21:00, local bands, fireworks. A council event for residents. 10–15% public-holiday surcharge; some closures. | **A non-event, and that's fine.** If the reef block owns 18–31 Jan, take a rest day. Don't restructure to be here; don't restructure to leave. |
| 5 | **Adelaide** | Survival Day Tandanyangga, Victoria Square 12:00–17:00; dawn smoking ceremony at Elder Park; free Aus Lights on the River in the evening. | Respectable, entirely off-route. *(Note: the 2026 Aus Lights evening was cancelled for extreme heat — a live risk for any outdoor 26 Jan plan anywhere.)* |
| 6 | **Perth** | **☠️ Skyworks no longer exists.** Run 1985–2022, ended by the City of Perth on cost, environmental and waning-interest grounds. Since 2023: a smaller Langley Park event — family zone from 15:00, food trucks, BYO picnic zone from 18:30, and a 30-min **drone + fireworks** show from 20:30 synced to FM 89.3. | Answered for the record. Moot — they leave WA on 26 December. |
| 7 | **Hobart** | Small regattas on the Derwent. Tasmania's real maritime day is **5–8 Feb**. | Not a reason to be there, not a reason to avoid it. |

### Verdict

**Do not move the trip for Australia Day.** It is a soft anchor and every candidate is free — there is no spend to optimise, only placement.

- **If the flow lands them in Melbourne:** go, and go for **Share the Spirit**, not the tennis. Add the AO ground pass as a cheap afternoon if he wants it.
- **If the reef block owns 18–31 Jan:** stay put in Cairns/Port Douglas. Lose nothing that matters.
- **If Byron is in play, shift the block ~3 days earlier** — start **Sat 23 / Sun 24 Jan** instead of 27 Jan. That buys **Bangalow Market (24th)** + Australia Day in the hinterland + **Nimbin Market (31st)**, and it costs nothing.
- **Ruled out:** returning to Sydney. Best event in the country, still the wrong call.
- **Watch:** if **Frequency Forest** announces a January 2027 edition, the whole question reopens — that would be a psytrance camping festival on the Australia Day weekend two hours from Sydney.

### ⚠️ The Channon problem

**The Channon Craft Market is the flagship** — the original Northern Rivers counterculture craft market, handmade-only, festival atmosphere, direct descendant of the Aquarius-era scene. It is the single best market fit for her in the country.

**With the Byron block at 27 Jan – 2 Feb they miss every single occurrence:** 13 Dec they're in WA, 10 Jan is inside the reef window, 14 Feb is past the likely departure.

Two ways out:
1. **Accept Nimbin instead** — Sun **31 Jan** is a 5th Sunday, so Nimbin Markets run, and it already falls inside the proposed block. *This is free and requires no change. Protect it.*
2. **Move the Byron block to include Sun 10 Jan** — catches the Channon, but collides with the reef window (18–31 Jan) and would need the whole January half rebuilt.

Nudging Byron three days earlier (start 23/24 Jan) is the cheap middle: **Bangalow on the 24th, Nimbin on the 31st, Mullum Friday and Byron Artisan Saturday in between.**
