# Perth live music — Dec 2026 reality check

Research for [issue #55](https://github.com/kilbot/holidays/issues/55). Seeds the Perth live-music adventure ([#54](https://github.com/kilbot/holidays/issues/54)).

**Researched:** 27 August 2026. All sources accessed that day.
**Currency:** AUD. Costs per couple unless stated.
**Brief:** "rock and roll bands" at "really cool local music venues"; Paul remembers **The Bird** and **Mojo's** from years back. Base is **Mundaring Hills** (~35 km east of the CBD) — transport is a real constraint, see below.

---

## Headline

**Both of Paul's memories are still alive and still doing exactly what he remembers.** The Bird is gigging 4–6 nights a week with covers of **$12–21**; Mojo's is open **7 days, 3pm–late** with original music most nights. Neither is a nostalgia trap.

The casualties since he was last here are the *other* rooms: **The Sewing Room is dead** (Apr 2023), **Badlands has degraded from a venue into a promoter-hire shell**, and **Jack Rabbit Slim's died in Jan 2026** and is now a country bar. Any listing site still presenting those three as going concerns is stale — which is exactly the failure mode this project keeps hitting.

## Shortlist — the 5 rooms for his taste

Ranked for *local rock and roll in a cool room*, not for capacity.

### 1. The Bird — Northbridge · the answer to the brief
- **What:** 400-cap-ish, exposed brick, courtyard out back, the room where local bands cut their teeth. Genuinely unchanged in character.
- **Gig nights:** most nights — actual Aug–Oct spread includes **Wed, Fri, Sat, Sun** starts from 3pm to 8pm. Single launches and small tours.
- **Cover:** **$12–21**. Cheapest real gig in Perth.
- **Booking horizon:** ~5–6 weeks. December lineup won't publish until **late Oct / early Nov**.

### 2. Mojo's Bar — North Fremantle · the legacy room
- **What:** 250-cap, on the Stoned Crow site since the '70s. Garage/psych/experimental booking bias.
- **Gig nights:** **7 days, 3pm–late.** Weeklies worth knowing: **Mon** open mic ("Otherside of The Weekend", 5:30pm), **Wed** trivia 6pm ($10/table; first Wed of the month is MUSIQUIZ, $10pp) followed by the free **Songwriters Club**, **Thu** locals night with 5:30–6:30pm happy hour, **Fri** arvo piano sessions 5pm, **Sun** Soul Sunday 4pm.
- **Cover:** typically **$15–25** for a local bill; free for market/soul/open-mic sessions.
- **Note:** the Thursday locals discount and the free Sunday/Monday sessions make this the best *low-commitment* drop-in of the lot.

### 3. Indian Ocean Hotel — Scarborough · loudest and cheapest
- **What:** beachside pub band room doing straight-ahead rock and punk. Recent bills: Rose Tattoo, The Smith Street Band, The Buoys, Le Shiv.
- **Gig nights:** **Fri/Sat 8pm**, plus **Sunday Sesh 5:30pm for $5**.
- **Cover:** **$5–17 local, $29–56 touring.** The $5 Sunday sesh is the single best value in this document.
- **Catch:** Scarborough is a 45–55 min drive from Mundaring, wrong side of the city from Fremantle.

### 4. Rosemount Hotel — North Perth · the mid-size workhorse
- **What:** ~500 cap, renovated, free front-bar music alongside the ticketed band room. Perth's default step-up venue.
- **Gig nights:** gigs most Thu–Sat; weeklies include **Blue Tuesdays with DJ Joe West** and **Social Saturdays** ($2 oysters).
- **Cover:** **$30–90** ticketed; front bar free.
- **December, confirmed:** **Hugh Cornwell (The Stranglers) 9 Dec**, **KISS tribute by STRUTT'R 11 Dec ($30)**, **Thirsty Merc 19 Dec ($58)**.

### 5. Freo.Social — Fremantle · the sure thing
- **What:** 800-cap heritage artillery drill hall (the old Fly By Night), on-site brewery, big beer garden. Not intimate, but the **only venue with December already densely booked**.
- **Gig nights:** "every week" — in practice Thu–Sun, plus midweek touring.
- **Cover:** **$30–70** typical.
- **December, confirmed:** Saint Etienne 1 Dec · **The Rubens 3 Dec** · **Freo Rocks tribute fest 4 Dec** · Busby Marou 12 Dec · Jamie Webster 13 Dec · **Thirsty Merc 18 Dec**.

**Wildcard if he wants it heavier:** **Lynott's Lounge** (Northbridge, 200 cap) is the punk/metal room — Negative Approach and Tigers Jaw are on its Oct–Nov books.

## December note — what actually happens to the circuit

**Short answer: festival season neither drains nor floods the local rooms. It bifurcates them.**

What the December listings show as at 27 Aug 2026 (X-Press + venue pages):

| Venue | Dec 2026 gigs already listed |
|---|---|
| Freo.Social | **6** |
| Rosemount | 2 (+ Hugh Cornwell 9 Dec via X-Press) |
| The Rechabite | 2 (Uncle Acid 8 Dec; Barefaced Stories 17 Dec) |
| Milk Bar | 1 (Hendrix/Doors tribute, 18 Dec, $30) |
| Amplifier Bar | 1 (Chasing Abbey, 14 Dec) |
| Mojo's | 1 (A Brit Invasion, 5 Dec) |
| Port Beach | 1 (Defected, 5 Dec) |
| The Bird | 0 |
| Indian Ocean Hotel | 0 |

Three things follow:

1. **The mid-size rooms fill first and fill with touring/tribute product.** Every December slot booked four months out is a national tour or a tribute act — the summer touring cycle *feeds* Freo.Social, Rosemount, Rechabite and Amplifier.
2. **The local rooms look empty because they book late, not because they go dark.** The Bird's horizon is ~5 weeks and Mojo's ~10; both have full August–October calendars. **Zero December listings today is a booking-lead-time artefact, not a closure signal.** Expect The Bird's December to publish around **late October**.
3. **Festival sideshows are the December upside.** **Spilt Milk Perth lands 20 Dec 2026** (Claremont Showground), **Wildlands Jan 2027** (Arena Joondalup) and **Foo Fighters 25 Jan 2027** (HBF Park). Perth's isolation means touring acts routinely play a club sideshow either side of a festival date — those announcements come **Oct–Nov**.

🔴 **Re-check date: first week of November 2026.** Pull The Bird's, Mojo's and the Indian Ocean Hotel's December calendars then, plus Spilt Milk sideshow announcements. Anything booked before then locks him into the touring-tribute lane, which is not what he asked for.

## Suggested adventure shape — "Perth live music night"

### Option A — **The Bird crawl** (the brief, answered literally)
Northbridge. Walk-up, no ticket needed most nights, William St is dense enough to bail into a second venue.

- The Bird (181 William) → The Rechabite (224 William, 200 m north) → Lynott's Lounge (100 Melbourne St, 400 m) if it's still going.
- **Best nights:** Fri or Sat. Wed also viable — The Bird books midweek.
- **Cost per couple:** covers 2 × $12–21 = **$24–42**; drinks ~$14/pint × 6 = **$84**; late food $30. **≈ $140–160 before transport.**

### Option B — **Fremantle night** (the atmospheric one)
Mojo's is the better *room*; Fremantle is the better *evening*.

- Dinner in Freo → Mojo's (North Freo) or Freo.Social depending on the bill.
- **Best nights:** **Thursday** (Mojo's locals night, happy hour 5:30–6:30) or **Sunday** (Soul Sunday, 4pm, free) if he wants it cheap and easy; Fri/Sat for a real gig.
- **Cost per couple:** Mojo's cover 2 × $15–25 = **$30–50**; Freo.Social ticketed 2 × $30–70 = **$60–140**; drinks $84; dinner $90. **≈ $200–320 before transport.**

### Option C — **$5 Sunday** (the cheap one)
Indian Ocean Hotel Sunday Sesh, 5:30pm, **$5 each**. Beachside, rock band, done by 9pm. **≈ $100 per couple all-in before transport** — and it's the only option that doesn't require a late-night return.

### 🚗 The Mundaring problem — read before costing anything

Mundaring is **~35 km / 40–50 min** from Northbridge and **~50 km / 55–65 min** from North Fremantle. There is **no train from Mundaring** — the nearest station is **Midland** (~20 min drive), and the last services back run well before a band finishes.

| Approach | Cost per couple | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Rideshare/taxi both ways to Northbridge | **~$120–170 return** | Doubles the cost of the night; surge on Fri/Sat |
| Drive + designated driver | $15 fuel + parking $10–20 | Cheapest, but someone stays dry |
| Drive to **Midland**, train in, **taxi home from Midland** | ~$12 fares + ~$40 taxi | Best compromise; the Midland line is the one to check |
| **Stay over in Northbridge/Freo** | $180–280/night | Turns a gig into a proper night out — worth it once |

**Total cost bands per couple, all-in:**

| | Local room (The Bird / Mojo's) | Mid-size touring (Freo.Social / Rosemount) |
|---|---|---|
| With rideshare both ways | **$260–330** | **$320–490** |
| Driving, one sober | **$165–190** | **$225–350** |
| With an overnight stay | **$400–520** | **$460–680** |

### Weekday locks

- **Mon** — Mojo's open mic only. Not a gig night.
- **Tue** — dead everywhere except Rosemount's Blue Tuesdays (DJ, free).
- **Wed** — The Bird books gigs; Mojo's trivia + free Songwriters Club. Cheap and low-key.
- **Thu** — Mojo's locals night (10% off + happy hour); touring shows start appearing.
- **Fri / Sat** — everything is on. **Lock the Perth music night to a Friday or Saturday** unless taking the cheap Wed/Sun options.
- **Sun** — Indian Ocean Hotel $5 sesh (5:30pm); Mojo's Soul Sunday (4pm, free). Both finish early — the only options that work without a late-night drive home.
